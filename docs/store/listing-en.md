## Description [2510/10000]

```
Tapomo is a free Windows app that quietly measures how fast you really type. It lives in the system tray, stays out of your way and, when you want, shows you how you're doing. If you'd like to get better, it also has typing lessons, daily challenges and a keycap-shaped mascot that reacts to your typing.

WHAT YOU SEE
Tapomo counts your typing bursts (stretches without long pauses, so thinking never hurts your score) and shows you:
• Your speed in wpm, live and on average, including the net speed (taking deletions off).
• Your speed record and your best run of characters in a row without deleting.
• Your days in a row meeting your daily goal.
• The hours of the day you type best and the apps you type in most.
• Tapomo, the mascot: it cheers your records, puts on a crown when you beat your best run without deleting, says "oops!" when you delete, and turns into Supermochi when you're on a roll.

PRACTISE
• Lessons in blocks: home, top and bottom row, capitals, numbers, punctuation, accents, common words and sentences. For Spanish, US English, French or German keyboards.
• Normal, Precision and Pace modes, and hands under the keyboard showing which finger to use.
• Text of the day, a text library and your practice of the day with the keys you find hardest.
• A 1-minute sprint and a finger trainer.
• Daily challenges set from your own average, so they are within reach whether you type slowly or fast.

LEVELS AND REWARDS
Typing, practising and completing challenges earn XP and raise your level. Each level unlocks a reward for Tapomo's wardrobe: colours, hairstyles, beards and accessories. There are also badges, weekly leagues based on your consistency, and streak protectors 🛡️ that save your days in a row if you miss one. Nothing is ever lost or locked.

PRIVACY
Tapomo never stores what you type. Each keystroke is turned into a category (letter, space, delete…) right away and the key itself is thrown away, so it cannot rebuild your texts. Password fields are ignored entirely, and password managers are excluded by default. Everything is saved only on your computer. Nothing leaves it unless you turn it on: anonymous stats and leagues with friends are optional and off by default. Start with Windows is optional too and begins switched off.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Tapomo is free software (GPL-3.0), with no ads and no accounts, and its code is on GitHub. It is made by one person, Pol Rué. If you like it, you can support it voluntarily on Ko-fi; everything stays free for everyone.
```
