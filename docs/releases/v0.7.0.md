**EN** — Support Tapomo: Tapomo is free, open source and ad-free, made by one person with lots of love. It now has its own space for support (Ko-fi or GitHub Sponsors) and, as a thank-you, a supporter code that dresses Tapomo in the «Patron» skin and gives you its badge. Nothing is ever locked: everything stays free for everyone.

Also:
- Supermochi: when you're on a roll, Tapomo briefly turns golden, with an aura and speed lines, and tells you so. With a hairstyle on, it gets flame hair.
- New hairstyles: fringe (level 16), quiff (19) and bob (22), in black, brown or blond.
- The wardrobe combines better: fringe with the ninja band, superhero with muscles, a better-placed lumberjack beard.
- Tapomo reacts to keyboard shortcuts, and recognises Word, Excel and PowerPoint ones in Spanish or English.
- New challenges: «Do a lesson» and «Use today the shortcut you learnt yesterday».
- Keyboard mashing doesn't count: «asdasdasd» or «aaaaaa» never raise your speed, records or streak.
- Lessons start gentler: two letters first, then three, four… with short texts at the beginning.
- The app has a sticky header with a menu to jump to each section, and the «your pace» and «record» marks show their value.
